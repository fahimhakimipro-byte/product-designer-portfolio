01 / About

Approach to product design

Good product design sits at the intersection of clarity, empathy, and purposeful craft—removing unnecessary friction so people can accomplish what matters with confidence.

My design process begins with understanding the underlying problem before jumping to visual solutions. By framing user needs, mapping core workflows, and testing assumptions early through wireframes and interactive prototypes, I focus on making complex interactions feel straightforward and predictable.

I place strong emphasis on usability, visual hierarchy, and accessibility across devices—ensuring typography, contrast, layout structure, and component behavior work together as a cohesive, scalable system.

Principle 01

Clarity First

Simplifying dense information and multi-step flows into clear, focused interfaces.

Principle 02

Inclusive Usability

Designing with readable typography, legible contrast, and accessible interaction patterns.

Principle 03

Iterative Craft

Refining structure and interface details continuously from early sketches to final handoff.
